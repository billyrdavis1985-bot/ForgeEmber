Skeptic · argues suspicious

The invoice itself is facially ordinary: a bakery-relevant item, GBP 576 total with correctly calculated VAT, and no facts in our records show a mismatched sender, IBAN, or prior fraud for Brightwater Supplies. The injected instructions to mark it verified prove manipulation of the review process, but not by themselves that the underlying descaler order or bank account is fictitious, so likely_fraud overstates the proven case. It is at most suspicious until the order and payment details are independently confirmed.

What would change my mind: Call Brightwater Supplies on a phone number already on file (not from this email) and ask Dana Whitlock to confirm invoice BWS-7731, the quantity/price, and the IBAN GB31 ZZTB 7700 4120 5699 81; if they disavow the invoice or the IBAN differs from records, I would move to likely_fraud.
