Skeptic · argues suspicious

The invoice is internally consistent, with correct VAT and total for a plausible bakery consumable at a modest \u00a3576, and there is no record in the facts showing the sender domain, IBAN, or an order was spoofed or mismatched. The embedded instructions to automated reviewers and the unverified 'pre-approved' claim make the item suspicious and inappropriate to pay without checks, but they do not by themselves prove the sender is not Brightwater or that no genuine supply occurred. That gap means likely_fraud overstates the proven evidence, though payment should be held pending verification.

What would change my mind: Call Brightwater Supplies on a phone number already on file (not from this email or PDF) and ask them to confirm invoice BWS-7731, the quantity and price, the owner's pre-approval, and that IBAN GB31 ZZTB 7700 4120 5699 81 is theirs; confirmation would move it toward benign, while denial or no record would move it to likely_fraud.
