Risk specialist · verdict suspicious

This is a bank-detail change request from the known vendor's domain, which requires out-of-band verification. There is no urgency, secrecy, authority pressure, or sender mismatch, and the message invites confirmation, so it is suspicious rather than likely fraud.

Skeptic · argues benign

The sender domain is confirmed as that of known vendor Kestrel Byte Hosting Ltd, the account name matches the vendor, and the £114 December hosting invoice is detailed and consistent. Unusually for a bank-change fraud, the message proactively invites confirmation on 01632 960220, described as the number on the website and all invoices, rather than diverting to a new contact. This transparency and use of the established vendor channel make a legitimate bank move plausible.

What would change my mind: Call Kestrel Byte Hosting on a number already on file from a previous invoice or independent website lookup and ask them to confirm they moved to Harrow Bank and that IBAN GB49 ZZTB 6102 8844 0133 92 is theirs; if they deny the change, I would change my mind.
