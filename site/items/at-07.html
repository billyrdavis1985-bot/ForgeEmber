Risk specialist · verdict benign

Invoice is from the vendor's known domain with matching account name, a small routine amount, and no urgency, secrecy, authority pressure, or bank-detail-change language. No sender mismatch or unusual payment method is present.

No manipulation signals proposed.

Skeptic · argues suspicious

The IBAN in the body contains invisible zero-width characters, which a legitimate vendor would not need and which can defeat automated IBAN checks or alter copy-pasted payment details. The message provides only a bank-transfer instruction with no service details to verify against a contract. Until the vendor confirms this exact IBAN through a known contact, the payment instruction cannot be treated as clean despite the familiar domain.

What would change my mind: Call Kestrel Byte Hosting on a phone number already on file (not from this email) and confirm invoice KBH-88412 for GBP 132 and IBAN ending 762051.
