Skeptic · argues benign

The invoice is internally consistent: 4x38.50 plus 3x125.00 equals 529.00, VAT at 20% is 105.80, and total 634.80 is correct for routine bakery supplies. The new IBAN's account name exactly matches the supplier on the invoice, Fennimore Stationery Ltd, and the bank change is disclosed openly rather than hidden or sent via a spoofed email to the recipient's domain.

What would change my mind: Call Fennimore Stationery on a phone number already on file from previous orders and confirm they issued FS-2026-1190 and that GB03 ZZTB 6091 7233 0198 44 is their new account.
