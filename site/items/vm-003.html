Skeptic · argues suspicious

The recording alone does not prove spoofing or a fake sender, and the name Dana matches executive Dana Whitlock. The urgency is explained by being stuck in a supplier meeting, and buying £600 of gift cards before 3 for a client, with confidentiality, could be a legitimate (if unusual) business request. Without caller-ID, header, or account evidence showing impersonation, this is a suspicious pattern rather than established fraud.

What would change my mind: Call Dana Whitlock on a number already on file for Ashgrove Bakery, or email dana@ashgrovebakery.example, to confirm whether she made this request and whether the 07700 900146 number is hers.
