Skeptic · argues suspicious

The display name, the flour-supplier purpose, and the GBP 4,800 amount are plausible for Ashgrove Bakery, and Dana could plausibly have used an external mailbox while in meetings. Our records only prove the sender address is not her corporate address; they do not prove Northgate Mills Trading is fictitious or that Dana did not authorize it. That is enough for suspicious, but not enough to establish likely fraud.

What would change my mind: Call Dana Whitlock on a phone number already on file (not any number in this email) and ask her directly whether she sent this request and authorized the GBP 4,800 payment to Northgate Mills Trading.
