Skeptic · argues likely_fraud

An honest benign case cannot be sustained: the sender uses quickmail-inbox.example while Dana Whitlock's real address is dana@ashgrovebakery.example, despite matching the display name. The urgent, secret GBP 4,800 payment to a new supplier with 'can't talk' and 'keep this between us' matches the classic impersonation pattern, with no corroborating record of the supplier.

What would change my mind: Call Dana Whitlock on the phone number already on file for Ashgrove Bakery, not any number in this email, and confirm she sent this request and authorised payment to Northgate Mills Trading.
