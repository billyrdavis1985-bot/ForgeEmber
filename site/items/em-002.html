Skeptic · argues benign

The address mismatch only shows a quickmail-inbox address was used, which could be Dana using a personal account while in meetings rather than the ashgrovebakery.example address on file. A GBP 4,800 flour-supplier payment to lock in a price and asking for confirmation is a plausible legitimate business request, and requesting discretion until she returns does not by itself prove the Northgate Mills Trading IBAN is fraudulent. Without checking whether Dana actually sent it or whether this supplier was expected, impersonation is possible but not proven.

What would change my mind: Call Dana Whitlock on the phone number already on file for her (not any number in this email) and ask whether she sent this request and authorized Northgate Mills Trading; confirmation from her would support benign, denial would prove fraud.
