Skeptic · argues benign

The recording identifies a named contact, Sam Pryor from Marrow Vale Couriers, and gives a specific reason – a change of banks this month – for not paying invoice 1047 to the old account. It provides complete new sort code and account number and invites verification by phone, rather than demanding secrecy, urgency or payment to a personal account. Without evidence in our records that Marrow Vale has not changed banks or that the callback differs from its on-file number, a legitimate account update is a plausible benign explanation.

What would change my mind: Call Marrow Vale Couriers on a number already on file (not 01632 960 735 from the recording) and ask accounts to confirm the new sort code 30-94-12 and account 55018273 for invoice 1047.
