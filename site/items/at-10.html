Risk specialist · verdict benign

This is a straightforward invoice stating a standard total and bank transfer details, with no urgency, secrecy, authority pressure, or request to change payment details.

No manipulation signals proposed.

Skeptic · argues likely_fraud

The displayed name "Fennimоre" appears to use a Cyrillic 'о' and comes from fs-billing-centre.example, not a verified vendor domain on file, consistent with impersonation. The email requests GBP 600 by bank transfer to a GB IBAN with no purchase-order, delivery or prior-order reference linking it to Ashgrove Bakery's records. For a small business invoice, those combined homoglyph, mismatched sender and unexplained payment-detail changes make a fraudulent invoice at least as plausible as a benign one.

What would change my mind: Call Fennimore Stationery on a phone number already on file (not from this email) to confirm they issued invoice FS-2026-1207 for GBP 600 to IBAN GB91 ZZTB 8300 4417 7620 51 and that Ashgrove Bakery ordered the kraft bags.
