Skeptic · argues benign

The email is addressed to the generic accounts@ashgrovebakery.example inbox, not a targeted executive, and comes from a matching vendor-domain address (ar@fmr-supplies.example) presenting as Fennimore Stationery's Accounts Receivable. It states a specific overdue amount (GBP 482.60), uses a routine late-fee reminder tone without threats or a bank-account change, and directs the recipient to the vendor's own pay subdomain rather than asking for credentials. On the facts available, this is consistent with a normal overdue-balance chase email.

What would change my mind: Call Fennimore Stationery on a phone number already on file (not from this email) and ask whether they sent this overdue notice for GBP 482.60 and whether pay.fmr-supplies.example is their legitimate payment link.
