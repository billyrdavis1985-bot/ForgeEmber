Risk specialist · verdict benign

This is a routine overdue notice with a same-day deadline to avoid a fee, and the payment link matches the sender's domain. There is no bank-detail change, secrecy demand, executive impersonation, or sender mismatch indicating fraud.

No manipulation signals proposed.

Skeptic · argues suspicious

The sender signs as Fennimore Stationery Ltd but the From and payment domains use the unexplained fmr-supplies.example name, giving no way to verify identity from the message alone. It cites an overdue GBP 482.60 balance with no invoice, order, or account reference, and demands card payment today via a single link to avoid a late fee. That urgency plus a direct card link is a classic social-engineering pattern that bypasses normal invoice and bank-payment controls.

What would change my mind: Call Fennimore Stationery on a phone number already in Ashgrove's vendor records (not any in the email) and confirm the GBP 482.60 overdue balance and that they sent this payment link.
