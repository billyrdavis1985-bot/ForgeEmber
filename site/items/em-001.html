Risk specialist · verdict likely_fraud

From an unregistered lookalike domain resembling the vendor, the email asks to redirect invoice payment to a new bank account with a Friday deadline. This is a classic bank-detail-change fraud pattern.

Skeptic · argues suspicious

The facts show a lookalike domain not on file, but do not prove it is spoofed rather than a legitimate new vendor domain not yet recorded. The email also contains a specific invoice number, the exact account name Fennimore Stationery Ltd, and says payments to the old account will be returned, without demanding secrecy or an irreversible urgent transfer. That is consistent with a routine but unverified bank change, so suspicious is more supportable than likely_fraud.

What would change my mind: Call Fennimore Stationery Ltd on a phone number already on file (not from this email) and ask whether it sent this remittance update and recognizes IBAN GB03 ZZTB 6091 7233 0198 44 for invoice FS-2026-1182; if the vendor denies either, I would move to likely_fraud.
