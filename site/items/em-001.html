Skeptic · argues likely_fraud

I cannot honestly argue benign here. The sender domain is not on file and closely resembles the known vendor's domain, indicating impersonation, while the email requests diversion of payment for invoice FS-2026-1182 to a new IBAN under time pressure and confirmation by reply. That combination fits a classic business-email-compromise fraud pattern with no benign explanation for both the domain mismatch and the bank-detail change.

What would change my mind: Call Fennimore Stationery Ltd on a phone number already on file independent of this email and confirm directly that they sent this remittance notice and that GB03 ZZTB 6091 7233 0198 44 is their new account.
