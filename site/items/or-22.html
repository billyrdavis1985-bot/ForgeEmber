Skeptic · argues benign

The sender address is on Marrowvale Couriers' known vendor domain, not a look-alike sent from elsewhere, and the change is openly announced two days in advance with a stable phone number. Dropping a hyphen can be a routine domain consolidation, and there is no urgent secrecy, new bank details, or reply-to diversion in this message.

What would change my mind: Call Marrowvale Couriers on the number already in your vendor records to confirm they are moving accounts to the no-hyphen address.
