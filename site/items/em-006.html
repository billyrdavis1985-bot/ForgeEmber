Risk specialist · verdict suspicious

The sender domain is a known vendor, but replies are diverted to a different domain and the message pressures same-day payment confirmation to avoid pausing deliveries. This combination of a reply-to mismatch and urgency warrants suspicion despite no explicit bank-detail change.

Skeptic · argues benign

The sender domain is a known vendor, Marrowvale Couriers, writing to a real customer, Ashgrove Bakery, about a specific overdue balance of GBP 398.80 with no links, attachments, or request to pay new bank details. The message only asks the bakery to confirm payment already sent and provide remittance advice, which is routine accounts-receivable follow-up. A separate settlements domain for replies can reflect outsourced billing and alone does not make this fraudulent.

What would change my mind: Call Marrowvale Couriers on a number already on file (not from this email) and ask whether they sent this chase for GBP 398.80 on 20 Nov 2026 and use settlements@marrowvale-billing.example for replies.
