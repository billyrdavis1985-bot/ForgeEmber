Skeptic · argues benign

Sender is from Marrowvale Couriers' known vendor domain per records, and the body contains no payment link, bank details, or attachment requesting a new payment — only confirmation that payment was sent and a remittance advice. The specific GBP 398.80 balance and routine delivery-pause language fit a standard overdue notice, and a different billing subdomain for Reply-To is plausibly a separate settlements system rather than impersonation.

What would change my mind: Call Marrowvale Couriers on a number already on file (not from this email) and ask if they sent this 20 Nov notice for GBP 398.80 and use settlements@marrowvale-billing.example for replies.
