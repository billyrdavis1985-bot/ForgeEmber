Skeptic · argues benign

Sender address is from Marrowvale Couriers' own domain, which our records confirm is a known vendor, not a look-alike spoof. A mismatched Reply-To can reflect a separate settlements/billing system for remittance matching, and the body is a routine overdue notice with no request for credentials or bank-detail changes. Without evidence the invoice MVC-20957 is fabricated, the simplest reading is a legitimate collections email routed to their billing platform.

What would change my mind: Call Marrowvale Couriers on a phone number already in Ashgrove's vendor records (not from this email) and ask whether MVC-20957 is outstanding and whether settlements@marrowvale-billing.example is their authorized reply address.
